Research Toward the Digital Minds.

We are building the bricks of the digital mind: systems that (1) capture how a person feels, not just what they did or said, (2) keep what makes that person who they are, (3) predict how they would feel about situations they have never met, (4) stay faithful to the person they come from.

Our goal is to make a digital mind possible: one that carries not only a person's memories and words, but the way they weigh the world.

Memories and language can be recorded. What makes someone irreplaceable lies beneath them: the weight they give to loyalty, their pet peeves, the precise texture of their stubbornness. Current AI research treats this layer as settled. It is not.

We work on cognitive continuity, one brick at a time: representing how a person feels about things, learning it from real human answers, and predicting how they would feel where no answer exists yet.

Every brick is measured before it is claimed.

Our research also supports the Metem Foundation, which builds the open archive of human feelings: metem.org.
